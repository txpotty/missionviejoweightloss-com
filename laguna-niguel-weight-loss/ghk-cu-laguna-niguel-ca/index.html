    How GHK-Cu Copper Peptide Therapy Works in Laguna Niguel, CA

    How GHK-Cu Works in Laguna Niguel

    GHK-Cu is glycine, histidine and lysine holding one copper ion. It was first identified in human plasma, where levels are higher in young adults and decline with age. In laboratory and animal work it appears to increase collagen and elastin production, speed wound repair and reduce inflammatory signals, possibly by carrying copper where tissue needs it. Most human data come from topical skincare studies.

    Who GHK-Cu Is Usually For

    Typical patients want help with skin texture, fine lines, thinning hair or general tissue recovery. Loose skin after weight loss is a common reason. People with Wilson disease, a copper allergy, active cancer, or who are pregnant or breastfeeding are generally not candidates, and it does not cause weight loss.

    GHK-Cu Dosing and Schedule in Laguna Niguel

    Two forms, different routines. Injections are commonly 1 to 2 milligrams subcutaneously, given daily or a few times a week in cycles of roughly one to two months. Creams are applied to clean, dry skin morning and night or once daily. Your provider sets the exact amount and cycle length, and you get written storage and mixing instructions with the shipment.

    What to Expect Over Twelve Weeks

    Little happens in the first two weeks. Collagen remodeling is slow, so visible skin changes typically appear somewhere between week four and week twelve, if they appear at all. Hair and scalp effects take longer, often three months or more. A full cycle comes before any verdict.

    GHK-Cu Side Effects in Laguna Niguel

    Stinging is the most common complaint. Redness, itching and a bruise-like bluish tint where you inject can also happen, and the tint usually fades within days. Creams may dry out or irritate sensitive skin, and occasional headache or tiredness has been reported. Long, high-dose use could in theory push copper too high, so your provider keeps cycles short.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review covers goals, medical history and supplements. You will be asked about Wilson disease, liver disease, allergies, cancer and pregnancy plans, and about any zinc or copper supplements because they change copper balance. Some cases get a copper or liver panel. Photos of your skin can be uploaded.
